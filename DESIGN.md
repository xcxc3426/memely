# A bright newsroom for meme culture

Memely uses a cool-white canvas, a navy masthead, hot-pink narrative marks, blue activity marks and electric-lime highlights. Heavy sans-serif headlines give the source desk an editorial hierarchy without reusing a dark trading terminal or paper journal layout.

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cool white | #fcfcff | Canvas |
| Navy | #101427 | Masthead, primary research blocks |
| Hot pink | #f53f93 | Narrative attention and source accents |
| Blue | #496df5 | Supplied activity context |
| Lime | #e6ff60 | Research status and highlighted questions |

The mascot is an original pixel Shiba Inu with a familiar meme side-eye. It appears at the top of the README, in the cover and within the app. It is not a numerical encoding or provider logo.

Each view has its own composition: narrative cards, a bipartite map, a source thread, quote cards, time bins, a matrix, activity tiles, a two-lens comparison, repeat exclusions, an AI evidence contract, a dispatch and a digest field.

The source interface includes text alternatives for every selected narrative's retained current posts. Canvas source views do not replace the full JSON and text export for accessibility or detailed inspection.
